# TV Home identity

TV Home is a quiet, personal home for the television. The mark puts three rounded cards inside a TV frame, echoing the clock, weather, and app layout. A small stand keeps the symbol recognizable as a television at icon sizes.

- **Name:** TV Home
- **Tagline:** Your space. On screen.
- **Charcoal:** `#161618`, the primary surface.
- **Porcelain:** `#F4F1ED`, the mark and wordmark.
- **Ash:** `#A6A3A5`, secondary elements.
- **Ember:** `#A84E55`, a restrained accent inspired by the wallpaper.
- **Typography:** a bold sans-serif wordmark with light, spacious supporting text. The editable banner uses Arial with a sans-serif fallback.

## Assets

- `tv-home-mark.svg`: transparent symbol.
- `tv-home-icon.svg` and `tv-home-icon.png`: square app icon.
- `tv-home-banner.svg` and `tv-home-banner.png`: 16:9 banner for Android TV and the repository.

Keep generous clear space around the symbol. Use the banner for wide placements and the icon for square placements; do not stretch one into the other. The launcher itself keeps its existing layout, monochrome accents, and dimmed wallpaper.
